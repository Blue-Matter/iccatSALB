# iccatSALB: South Atlantic Albacore Management Strategy Evaluation

Operating models, management procedures, and analysis tools for the
Management Strategy Evaluation (MSE) of South Atlantic albacore tuna,
developed for the International Commission for the Conservation of
Atlantic Tunas (ICCAT).
